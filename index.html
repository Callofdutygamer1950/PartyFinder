About & Rules

What is Drop the Addy?

An anonymous, live map of what's happening around Slippery Rock. Anyone can report whether a spot is 🎉 Party Time or 💀 Dead, vote on locations, and leave short comments. No account needed.

How statuses work

  • 🎉 Party Time: one report is enough to mark a spot as live.
  • 💀 Dead takes 3 reports from 3 different people, so one person can't shut a party down on their own.
  • 👍/👎 votes are one per person per spot, and all votes reset every day at 8am.
  • 🔥 "reports in the last hour" counts how many different people updated a spot recently.

Community rules

  • No slurs, threats, harassment, or personal information about anyone.
  • Don't post fake reports or propose places that aren't real.
  • Stay safe: see the Get Home Safe numbers on the main page or get a DD from Snapchat.

What we store

  • An anonymous ID for your browser (no name, email, or phone number). It's what lets us limit votes and reports per person.
  • Your reports and votes, linked to that anonymous ID. Votes are cleared every morning at 8am.
  • Comments and any tags you add. Comments are deleted after 12 hours.
  • Proposals, bug reports and feature requests you submit.
  • Your location is never stored or sent anywhere. "Near me" only uses it inside your own browser to sort by distance.

The database is backed up nightly and backups are kept for 30 days.

🔔 Getting party alerts

Open a party and tap Subscribe to get a notification when it turns 🎉 Party Time or is reported 💀 Dead.

  • Android: Chrome works best, and alerts arrive even when it's closed. For an app-like experience, tap Install App at the bottom of the page.
  • Firefox on Android: alerts may wait until you open Firefox. To fix that, go to Settings → Apps → Firefox → Battery and choose Unrestricted.
  • iPhone / iPad: in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen, open Drop the Addy from the new icon, then subscribe. iOS only sends alerts to installed web apps.
  • Computer: works in Chrome, Edge and Firefox while the browser is running.

Alerts are only sent when a status actually changes. Unsubscribe any time with the same button.

Questions or problems?

Use the Request Feature / Report Bug button at the bottom of the page.
