Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

Short version: Drop the Addy has no accounts and never asks for your name, email, phone number or location. Your browser gets an anonymous ID so we can limit votes and reports to one per person. Comments are deleted after 12 hours. Nothing is sold, and there are no ads or tracking.

Who runs this app

Drop the Addy is an independent, student-run project for the Slippery Rock area. It is not affiliated with Slippery Rock University. The website and the Android app are the same service and follow this policy.

What we collect

  • An anonymous ID. When you open the app, you're signed in anonymously through Firebase Authentication. This random ID isn't linked to your name, email or phone. It's used to stop the same person voting or reporting repeatedly.
  • Status reports and votes you make (Party Time or Dead, optional crowd estimate, 👍/👎), stored with that anonymous ID and the time.
  • Comments and tags you post, and flags you put on other comments.
  • Party proposals, bug reports and feature requests you submit, including the map location you pick for a proposed party.
  • A notification token, only if you subscribe to party alerts, plus the list of parties you subscribed to.
  • Admin accounts: the people who run the app sign in with an email and password. Visitors never need an account.

What we don't collect

  • Your location. "Near me" uses your device's location only inside your browser to sort parties by distance. It is never saved or sent to us.
  • Your name, email address, phone number, contacts or photos.
  • Advertising IDs, analytics or tracking. There are no ads.

How long we keep it

  • Comments, their tags and flags: deleted after 12 hours.
  • Votes: reset every day at 8am.
  • Status report history used for admin statistics: kept for about 30 days.
  • Parties listed for a set time: deleted, with their comments and votes, when the time is up.
  • Notification tokens: until you unsubscribe or the token stops working.
  • Nightly backups of the database are kept for 30 days and then deleted.

Services we use

These providers process data on our behalf to run the app:

  • Google Firebase (database, anonymous sign-in, push notifications).
  • Google Apps Script and Google Drive (automatic clean-up jobs and nightly backups).
  • GitHub Pages (hosting the website).
  • OpenStreetMap (map images) and unpkg / Google's CDN (code libraries). Like any website, these see your IP address when your device loads files from them.
  • Discord (private alerts to the admins). New proposals, bug reports, feature requests and comments caught by moderation are sent there for review.

Hosting and service providers may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, under their own privacy policies. We don't sell or share your data with anyone else.

Content you post

Comments, reports, votes and approved party listings are public to everyone using the app. Don't post personal information about yourself or anyone else. Comments that break the community rules may be hidden or deleted by admins or by the automatic word filter.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from alerts any time with the same Subscribe button, or turn off notifications in your device settings.
  • Clearing the app's or site's data gives you a new anonymous ID and removes the votes and subscriptions remembered on your device.
  • To ask for something you posted to be removed, use Report Bug in the app and describe it.

Age

Drop the Addy is intended for adults (18+). It isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Use the Request Feature / Report Bug button at the bottom of the app.
